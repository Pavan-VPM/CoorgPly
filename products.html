Plywood Grades

Every panel IS-certified, double-pressed, and GLP-treated for termite immunity.

IS:303

MR Grade

Commercial plywood with melamine-urea bonding. Ideal for dry interior applications.

Thickness: 4–19mm

Warranty: 15 Years

Best for: Wardrobes, partitions

IS:303 WP

BWR Grade

Phenol-formaldehyde bonding withstands boiling water exposure and high humidity zones.

Thickness: 6–25mm

Warranty: 20 Years

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms

IS:710

BWP Marine

Un-extended phenol glue, triple-treated microbe shield. The ultimate structural panel.

Thickness: 6–19mm

Warranty: Lifetime

Best for: Marine, exterior
